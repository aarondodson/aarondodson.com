Experian Product Design Strategy

Design leadership · Insurance Marketplace

I led UX design for Experian’s Insurance Marketplace launch. The work centered on helping people navigate a complicated decision while supporting a new area of growth for the business.

The challenge

Insurance asks people to make sense of unfamiliar information before they can confidently take the next step. Our job was to make the experience easier to navigate and give the business a clear way to evaluate it.

What I led

I set design direction with designers, content specialists, and researchers, aligned priorities with cross-functional partners, and presented the strategy to executives.

How we evaluated the work

We used research and analytics to understand where people struggled and to guide design priorities. The Insurance Marketplace launched as part of Experian’s expansion into new categories.
